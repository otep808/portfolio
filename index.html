★ Project of the Day

EkSpyRy — Food Expiration Monitor

A Python desktop app that monitors food expiry dates and fires alerts before items spoil. Built because food waste is not a feature.

You log each item with its expiry date, and the app keeps the list saved on your computer. It checks the dates every time you open it and flags what’s about to expire — no account, no cloud, just your fridge, digitised.
